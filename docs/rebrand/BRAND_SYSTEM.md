- Wax foreground: `#e8e5ea`; muted foreground: `#9e98a3`.
- Obsidian Brass identity: `#b89a61`; highlight: `#d4bd89`.
- Mineral violet interaction: `#75617e`; thinking/Pulse: `#9a82a5`.
- Orange and bright honey yellow are retired from the Mitsuro product palette.

## Material

The launcher mark is a concave cell cast into black metal, centered on a full-bleed graphite shell. Metallic contrast comes from neutral graphite/pewter values. Brass is intentionally absent as a visible shimmer on the launcher master.
